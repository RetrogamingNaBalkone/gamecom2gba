# Changelog

## 0.1.1

Duke Nukem 3D fixes since v0.1 apply to both campaign builds.

- Restored the cartridge music tempo of 25 ms per note unit. In episode 2, the second voice now rests until the music loops instead of playing notes from episode 3.
- Corrected the timing of the first enemy strike when Duke closes to melee range in a corridor, including after an enemy recovers from a hit.
- Fixed missing graphics in the Duke title animation. Pressing A or Start now advances the opening one screen at a time through the logo, copyright notice, title animation, and main menu.
- Restored the original pause prompt, “Press PAUSE to continue.” Press Start on the GBA to resume. The checkpoint password appears on the death screen.
- Changed the internal GBA ROM title from `DUKE PREVIEW` to `DUKE-GAMECOM`.
